# OAK-1 / B601 mount: 1 mm tighter anti-rotation bracket

The single upright anti-rotation wall is moved 1 mm toward the tripod hole:
its inner face changes from CAD Y=24 to Y=23 mm. The perpendicular distance
from the hole centre to the wall changes from 19 to 18 mm.

The offset tripod hole remains at X=12.75, Y=5 mm, diameter 6.8 mm. The wall's
3.5 mm thickness and Z=44 mm top are unchanged. Seating plate, arm attachment,
and print orientation are unchanged. Older designs are preserved separately.

Print OAK1_B601_snug_1mm_PRINT.stl in its supplied long-side-down orientation,
using the same settings and hardware as the previous offset-hole mount.
Test camera fit before tightening; the requested 1 mm adjustment has not been
physically tested. CAD and closed-manifold mesh checks passed, including an
unchanged lower mount and clear tripod bore.

Editable STEP and build_snug_camera_mount.py are included. The script uses the
previous OAK1_B601_offset_tripod.step as its input. Original source chain and
Seeed reference are included in the bundle. Derived design: CERN-OHL-W-2.0.
